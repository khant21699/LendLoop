# LendLoop surfaces (home, browse, requests, listings)

Scope and mode: whole app. Home is Persuade; Browse, Requests, Listings are Operate. Redesign of the incumbent look; product truth, copy intent, function and the pinned palette stay.

Audience and job: neighbors (here, a hiring team reviewing a fictional neighborhood board) finding a tool, asking for dates, approving, returning.

## Direction contract

THESIS: LendLoop is a drawing sheet. Every tool is a labelled part and every loan a stamped revision. Refuses the category default of rounded white cards with soft shadows and a pill button.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned slate and indigo tokens (extended with a hairline indigo grid tint and a pale indigo wash). Faint 24px construction grid on the page ground, hairline rules instead of boxes, 2px corner radius on controls, indigo ink. Bricolage Grotesque display, Geist body, Geist Mono for part data, dates and labels only. Authored line-drawing icons per category, one stroke weight. Status shown as ink-stamp badges. A title block closes each sheet.

STORY: A visitor sees a friendly, slightly cheeky sheet that says neighbors lend tools here, understands the available tools at a glance as a parts list, and signs in to lend or borrow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home. Left two-thirds: LendLoop in large display type, the tagline beneath, and the "Sign in to lend or borrow" primary button. Right third: a line-drawn hammer with dimension lines and one playful callout, drawing itself in once on load. Below the fold line: the available-tools parts list in two hairline columns. Header is a thin sheet bar with wordmark left, nav and theme toggle right.

FORM: The Drafting Sheet, assigned (index 7 of my list); seed key d74cd241. Raised by the Crouwel grid (visible grid is structural, spacing snaps to its unit) and the design annual plate section (hairlines and small mono labels replace boxes and shadows).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
